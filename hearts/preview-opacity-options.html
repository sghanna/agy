WCAG Guidance Insight: WCAG 2.1 SC 1.4.3 requires 3.0:1 for large text (≥18pt or ≥14pt bold) and SC 1.4.11 requires 3.0:1 for graphical controls. Mom's cards use heavy 24–28px bold Didone numerals, putting the minimum passing threshold at 3.0:1.

Notice that ALL FOUR options (20% through 35%) comfortably exceed 3.0:1 for both red ink and black ink! Even at 35%, red contrast is 3.84:1 (28% above standard). At 20%, red contrast is 4.51:1, meeting the strict standard for standard body copy!
