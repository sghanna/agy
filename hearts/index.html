Official Rules of Hearts

Goal: Keep your score as low as possible. First player to reach 100 points ends the match; lowest score wins.

Penalty Points: Each Heart is worth 1 point (13 total). The Queen of Spades (Q♠) is worth 13 points. Total = 26 points per hand.

Passing Rotation: Hand 1: Pass Left · Hand 2: Pass Right · Hand 3: Pass Across · Hand 4: Hold (no passing). Cycle repeats.

Official Rules:

  • Opening Lead: The player holding the 2 of Clubs (2♣) MUST lead it to open the very first trick of the hand.
  • Strict Follow-Suit: You must follow the suit led if you hold cards in that suit.
  • No Blood on Trick 1: If void in clubs on Trick 1, you cannot discard Hearts or the Queen of Spades (unless holding only penalty cards).
  • Hearts Breaking: Hearts cannot be led until "broken" by an off-suit discard (or if you hold only Hearts).
  • Queen of Spades: Worth 13 points; does not break hearts; can be led anytime spades are legal.
  • Winning Tricks: Highest card of the led suit takes the trick. No trumps. The trick winner leads the next trick.
  • Shooting the Moon: If a player takes all 26 penalty points in a round, that player gets 0 points and all 3 opponents receive +26 points!
